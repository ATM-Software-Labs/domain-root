01Data controller

The controller of personal data collected through trujillomingorance.com and its subdomains is Alberto Trujillo Mingorance (TrujilloMingorance Labs).

For any question about data protection or to exercise your rights, write to alberto@trujillomingorance.com.

02Core privacy principles

  • No sale of data: we never sell, rent or share your personal data, usage history, prompts or documents with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No training of public models: requests, text, queries and code you send to our AI tools are not used to train public foundation models.
  • Data minimisation: we only ask for and process what is strictly needed to provide the service or authenticate you.

03Data we receive and third-party sign-in (OAuth 2.0)

Federated sign-in with trusted providers avoids extra passwords:

  • Sign-in with X (Twitter): through the official X API v2 we receive only your unique account id, public name, handle (@usuario) and public profile-image URL. We cannot read private messages or post to your account.
  • Sign-in with Google: through Google Identity we receive your Google id, email address and public name.
  • Direct email registration: if you register directly, the password is processed with modern salted cryptography (PBKDF2 / SHA-256). We never store plaintext passwords.

04Why we process data

We process data for these legitimate purposes:

  • Manage your session and let you move between ecosystem platforms (SSO).
  • Personalise your workspace and keep technical preferences, favourites and projects.
  • Send strictly transactional email (account confirmation, key recovery) with no advertising trackers or pixels.
  • Protect the infrastructure against attacks, quota abuse and unauthorised access with Zero Trust controls.

05Ephemeral inference and AI processing

In the AI tools (Trujillo AI Studio and Rewrite AI), inference follows ephemeral memory:

  • Traffic to language models uses TLS 1.3 encrypted streaming (Server-Sent Events) in volatile memory.
  • Documents and projects in your panel are stored first in browser storage (localStorage / IndexedDB).
  • You can clear memory, delete chats or purge history at any time from the application settings.

06Cookies and local storage

The platforms use only strictly necessary cookies and technical storage:

  • Federated session cookies: names such as ta_session, atm_session or session_token on Domain=.trujillomingorance.com with HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax. They keep your session across guides, ai, focusguard and the rest of the cluster.
  • Short-lived OAuth state cookies: used during PKCE to prevent cross-site request forgery (CSRF). They are deleted when login completes.
  • No third-party advertising cookies: we do not use ad networks or invasive fingerprinting or remarketing tools.

07Security and edge architecture

The infrastructure runs on Cloudflare’s global edge network, with these controls:

  • Strict transport encryption with TLS 1.3 and HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security).
  • Isolation headers: Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff and X-Frame-Options: DENY, against injection and clickjacking.
  • Cryptographic token checks at the edge, without exposing internal databases to the public network.

08Your data-protection rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable law, you can exercise:

  • Access: know which personal data we process about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your account and associated records.
  • Objection and restriction: object to processing or ask us to limit it.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used electronic format.

To exercise any of these rights at no charge, email the request to alberto@trujillomingorance.com. We will handle it as soon as we can.
