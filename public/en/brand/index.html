Design system

Public contract for the apex and the subdomains, starting with labs. Link these files; do not copy the values.

Logo

The symbol is the geometric mark from the avatar: a white line, with no accent color, at the same weight as the original drawing.

  • On a dark background, use the dark or white version. On a light background, use the light or black version.
  • The stroke is white on dark and ink on light. It has no accent color.
  • Labs uses the same mark. The word Labs is the only difference.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, or add a gradient, shadow, or outline.
  • Keep clear space equal to the symbol height. Do not draw it smaller than 16 px.
  • The hub wordmark is ATM Software Labs, set in JetBrains Mono Medium, with the capitals unchanged.

Usage

Responses for /brand/* and /css/site.css include Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *. On another origin, load fonts.css from /brand so font files are not requested from your host.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://trujillomingorance.com/brand/tokens.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://trujillomingorance.com/brand/fonts.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://trujillomingorance.com/css/site.css?v=20261015f">
